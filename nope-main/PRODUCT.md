# NOPE Main

## Register

brand

## Users

Ordinary people and small companies looking for a thoughtful, direct partner to design and build websites, apps, and other digital work. They should quickly understand who is behind the work, what NOPE makes, and whether the collaboration feels right.

## Product Purpose

Present NOPE as a one-person digital practice, show selected work, and make starting a conversation feel simple. Success means the site feels unmistakably human while making the work and offer easy to understand.

## Brand Personality

Human, minimal, alive. Direct without being cold, playful without becoming performative, and confident without corporate polish.

## Anti-references

Generic agency templates, anonymous corporate studio language, decorative complexity, and fashionable effects that make the work feel less personal or harder to understand.

## Design Principles

- Make the person behind the work visible.
- Cut anything that does not earn its place.
- Use interaction and motion to make the site feel cared for, not busy.
- Let distinctive choices carry personality without obscuring the message.
- Keep the path from curiosity to contact short.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core content and navigation should remain keyboard-operable, responsive, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences. Deliberately challenging visual experiments must stay optional and must not remove visible keyboard focus or access to the original presentation.
