# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors arriving from the nope digital site, Eddie's writing, or search. They're
curious about who is behind the work and the words — potential clients,
collaborators, and fellow builders reading on their own time.

## Product Purpose

blog.nope.digital is Eddie's personal corner: writing, experiments, and an
about page. It exists to show the person behind nope digital — the site itself
is a work sample. Success is a visitor leaving with a clear sense of a real,
specific person who makes things with care.

## Brand Personality

Honest, wry, handmade. First-person voice, short sentences, no jargon. The
sibling of nope.digital's "we're chasing soul / we say no a lot" tone, but
quieter and more personal — Eddie talking, not the studio pitching.

## Anti-references

- AI-buzzword portfolio copy ("passionate about crafting delightful digital
  experiences").
- Fragmented section-scaffold layouts: uppercase eyebrow labels above every
  block, card grids, hero-metric templates.
- Template portfolio sites that could belong to anyone.

## Design Principles

- One voice, one flow: pages read as a single piece of writing, not a stack of
  labeled sections.
- Type is the design: the ZT Nature display / Literata prose pairing carries
  the identity; big beats alternate with quiet ones.
- The off-white/off-black duo (`--2`/`--3`) is the canvas; color is rare and
  deliberate (brand purple as a signature, not decoration).
- Slight imperfection on purpose: gentle rotations and offsets, never grids of
  identical anything.
- Every word earns its place; compact beats complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text meets 4.5:1 on the off-white background. Skip link, focus
outlines, and reduced-motion alternatives already exist in the shared layout —
keep them intact on every page.
